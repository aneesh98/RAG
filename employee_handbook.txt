1. INTRODUCTION, MISSION & CORE VALUES
================================================================================

1.1 Welcome Message
Welcome to SynnoTech Innovations Inc. We engineer high-performance cloud infrastructure, 
resilient data pipelines, and intelligent enterprise software. Our success rests on 
the collective intellect, dedication, and integrity of our team. 

This handbook outlines the principles, mutual expectations, operational standards, 
and formal policies that govern your journey with SynnoTech. Please review it carefully.

- Autonomous Accountability: We grant broad operational autonomy to our employees 
  and trust them to deliver exceptional results with ownership and rigor.
- Integrity and Trust: We interact transparently with customers, teammates, and

2. EMPLOYMENT BASICS & WORK MODELS
================================================================================

2.1 Employment Classifications
- Full-Time Regular Employees: Individuals contracted to work at least 35 hours per 
  week on an indefinite basis. Fully eligible for corporate benefits, equity schemes, 
  and annual leave programs.
- Part-Time Regular Employees: Individuals scheduled for fewer than 30 hours per week. 
  Entitled to prorated statutory benefits and leave accruals.
- Independent Contractors & Temporary Staff: Specialized personnel engaged under 
  Statement of Work (SOW) agreements. Commercial terms, deliverables, and asset 
  allocations are established via vendor contract.

2.2 Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO)
SynnoTech provides equal employment opportunities to all team members and applicants 
without regard to race, color, ancestry, national origin, religion, sex, sexual 
orientation, gender identity or expression, age, disability, or veteran status.

2.3 Hybrid & Remote Work Expectations
SynnoTech operates under a flexible, hybrid-first posture. Team members are expected to:
- Maintain an ergonomic, quiet, and secure dedicated workspace.
- Adhere to core collaboration hours (10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time).
- Stay reachable via primary communication channels (Slack, Jira, and Email).
- Protect client and company data from unauthorized visual or digital access.

3. WORKPLACE SAFETY, HEALTH & HARASSMENT PREVENTION
================================================================================

3.1 Harassment & Non-Discrimination Policy
SynnoTech strictly enforces a zero-tolerance policy against sexual harassment, verbal 
hostility, physical intimidation, discriminatory slurs, bullying, or retaliatory actions. 
Violations lead to disciplinary intervention up to immediate summary dismissal.

Reporting Channels:
Concerns may be submitted directly to your direct manager, People Operations, or 
anonymously via our 24/7 whistleblower intake portal (compliance@synnotech.internal).

3.2 Workplace Safety & Ergonomics
Employees must adhere to safety compliance rules in physical corporate facilities, keep 
emergency fire pathways clear, and report any environmental hazards immediately. Remote 
employees may request ergonomic workstation assessments through People Operations.

3.3 Drug and Alcohol-Free Workplace
Consumption, possession, or distribution of illegal narcotics, or working while 
impaired by alcohol or controlled substances during business hours, is strictly prohibited.

4. PROFESSIONAL CODE OF CONDUCT & ETHICS
================================================================================

4.1 Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure
Employees encounter proprietary technology, source code, unreleased features, customer 
records, and financial metrics. All non-public information is confidential and must not 
be communicated externally without written authorization from Corporate Legal.

4.2 Intellectual Property Assignment
All software, scripts, documentation, algorithms, patentable discoveries, and 
trademarks conceived or developed during your employment with SynnoTech belong solely 
and exclusively to SynnoTech Innovations Inc.

4.3 Conflicts of Interest & Moonlighting
Employees must not engage in outside consulting, external commercial engagements, or 
advisory roles with competitors, vendors, or entities that interfere with their core 
duties or create actual or perceived conflicts of interest.

7.2 Cybersecurity & Equipment Standards
- Centralized Mobile Device Management (MDM): All devices are pre-configured with 
  SynnoTech's MDM software (Jamf/Microsoft Intune). Disabling, bypassing, or removing 
  MDM agents, profiles, or endpoint security monitors is a severe security violation.
- Full Disk Encryption: Enterprise-grade encryption (FileVault 2 / BitLocker) is active 
  by default. Encryption keys are escrowed in the corporate key vault.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Hardware-backed MFA or authenticator apps are 
  strictly mandatory for logging into all identity providers and cloud services.
- Prohibited Software: Employees must never install unapproved third-party utilities, 
  peer-to-peer file sharing platforms, unlicensed software, or cryptocurrency miners.
- Transit & Storage Security: Laptops must not be left unattended in parked vehicles, 
  coffee shops, or common hotel areas. Laptops transported in transit must be carried 
  in padded protective laptop bags.

8.2 Step-by-Step Reporting & Hot-Swap Dispatch Workflow
1. Immediate Safety Containment:
   If a device overheats, discharges smoke, exhibits a burning smell, or shows a 
   visibly bulging battery, unplug power immediately and move the device to a 
   fireproof, well-ventilated area. Do not attempt to reboot.
2. Submit Incident Ticket:
   Within 12 hours of an incident, submit a high-priority ticket via the IT Helpdesk 
   portal or email (it-emergency@synnotech.internal). Provide photos of the damage, 
   a brief description of what happened, and your confirmed shipping address.
3. Hot-Swap Replacement Dispatch:
   IT immediately provisions and ships an equivalent hot-swap workstation preloaded 
   with corporate security configurations and baseline software images.
4. Courier Return of Damaged Device:
   The employee will receive a shock-resistant return container with a prepaid priority 
   courier label. The damaged machine must be packaged and scanned with the carrier 
   within three (3) business days of receiving the replacement.
5. Technical Triage & Inventory Update:
   SynnoTech IT conducts internal diagnostics, recovers unbacked local files where 
   technically feasible, updates enterprise asset databases, and closes the ticket.

8.3 Replacement Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- Headquarters / On-site Hubs : Walk-up IT desk replacement in under 1 hour.
- Domestic Remote Personnel   : Next-Business-Day priority air courier dispatch.
- International Remote Staff  : 48 to 72 hours via DHL Express or FedEx Global.

9. PROGRESSIVE DISCIPLINE, RESIGNATION & ASSET RECOVERY
================================================================================

9.1 Progressive Discipline Framework
SynnoTech follows structured corrective steps for non-compliance or performance issues:
  Step 1: Informal Verbal Counseling
  Step 2: Formal Written Warning & Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
  Step 3: Final Written Reprimand & Final Probation
  Step 4: Separation / Employment Termination
The company reserves the right to initiate immediate termination in cases involving 
gross misconduct, illegal behavior, data breaches, or severe safety violations.

9.2 Resignation Notice Period
Exempt and salaried personnel are expected to provide at least two (2) weeks advance 
written notice to their manager and People Operations to facilitate handover.

9.3 Mandatory Asset Return Protocol
Departing employees must return all SynnoTech-owned property—including laptops, power 
cables, security keys, monitors, and building access cards—within five (5) business 
days of their final employment date. IT Logistics sends prepaid, insured return shipping 
crates directly to remote workers. Failure to return assigned assets may delay final 
clearances and result in asset recovery procedures.
